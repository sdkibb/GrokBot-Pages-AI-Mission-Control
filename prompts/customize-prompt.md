# Customize prompt

Paste this when you want a design tweak on an existing AI Mission Control Page, without disturbing the data.

```
Edit my AI Mission Control Page. Keep every data block exactly as it is, including the example-or-real numbers, bot list, dismissed items, and gap notes. Change only the design I describe below.

Keep these rules unless I explicitly override one of them:
- This week tiles stay visually neutral. Red and green are not used on those tiles.
- Red and amber are only for issues or elevation (highest usage, a high waiting count, or the projection chip).
- Waiting on you stays collapsed until I open it.
- Paused routines stay hidden until I toggle them.
- The More accordion still opens one section at a time.
- The weekly Now marker still comes from resetAt and moves by the hour.
- Pull-to-refresh and Refresh still redraw in place, with no full reload.

Here is the change I want:

[Describe the change.]
```
